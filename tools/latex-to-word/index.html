LaTeX to Word Converter

Convert LaTeX equations to Microsoft Word compatible format

How to use in Microsoft Word:

  • Click "Copy Output" to copy the converted equation
  • In Word, press Alt + = to insert an equation
  • Paste the copied text into the equation editor
  • Press Enter to convert it to a professional equation
